Create a modern, production-ready multi-tenant SaaS Point of Sale (POS) platform for web and mobile-responsive use. The system must support any business type including retail stores, groceries, pharmacies, hardware stores, restaurants, cafés, boutiques, convenience stores, salons, service businesses, and wholesalers.
